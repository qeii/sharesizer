## Your images stay on your device

Sharesizer's tools resize, crop, compress, convert and analyze images **in your browser**. The images you choose are not uploaded to our servers or to any third party.

## What is stored on your device

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. Nothing else is stored.

## Hosting and technical logs

This website is delivered by a hosting provider (Cloudflare). Like any host, it may process standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to serve the site and keep it secure. See Cloudflare's own privacy policy for details.

## Analytics, advertising and cookies

At the time of writing, Sharesizer does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If we add them in the future, for example advertising or analytics services that use cookies, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent first.

## Links to other sites

Some pages link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
