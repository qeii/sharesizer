Sharesizer is an independent project that makes everyday image tasks quick and private for content creators: resizing for social platforms, cropping, compressing, converting formats, editing photos, making QR codes, and testing YouTube thumbnails.

## Why it works the way it does

- **Private by design.** Everything runs in your browser. Your images are never uploaded.
- **No sign-up, watermarks or limits.**
- **Honest specs.** Platform sizes change often. Each size guide shows the date it was last checked, and where sources disagree we say so.

## Creator Guides

Alongside the tools, the [Creator Guides](/blog) share practical, honest advice for building a channel, including the real costs and timelines that many tutorials skip.

## Found a mistake?

If a size is out of date or a tool misbehaves, please tell us through the [contact page](/contact).
