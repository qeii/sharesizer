Snip to text

Drag a box around any text on your screen or in an image. It is read, shown below, and copied to your clipboard.

When your browser asks, pick the tab, window or screen that shows the text. You can also paste or drop a screenshot anywhere on this page.

Reading happens in your browser. The first read in each language downloads its language data once, so it needs an internet connection. Light text on a dark background is flipped automatically before reading.

Drag to select the text area. Press Esc to cancel.
Drop an image to read it
